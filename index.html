Lantern Road

A cozy walking game for Android and iPhone. Your real steps light the lantern.

You wake beside a fire. Behind you, the dark is coming. Far ahead, a pillar of light marks the summit. Lantern Road reads your step count from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone), so every walk you take carries your wanderer further down the road. Choose your road each morning, meet what waits on it, make camp at night and build up your Waystation.

Support

Questions, bugs or purchase problems: email kyleanthonypastor@gmail.com or post in #bug-reports on the Discord. Please include your phone model and the version shown in Settings.

My steps aren't showing up (iPhone)
Open the Health app, go to Sharing, then Apps, then Lantern Road, and turn on Steps. Steps from an Apple Watch or other apps that write to Apple Health count too.
My steps aren't showing up (Android)
Open Health Connect settings from the game's Settings, allow Lantern Road to read steps, and make sure your step tracker writes to Health Connect.
I bought embers but didn't get them
Reopen the game; purchases are checked again on launch. If they still haven't arrived, email the address above with the time of purchase.
Can I use miles?
Yes. Turn on Distances in miles in Settings.
Does it work on a treadmill?
A watch or fitness band counts treadmill steps far better than a phone in your hand.

Privacy

Lantern Road reads only your step count. No ads, no tracking, and the camp circle and local weather are optional. Read the full privacy policy.
