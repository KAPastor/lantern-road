Lantern Road privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

What the app reads

With your permission, Lantern Road reads your step count from Health Connect on your Android phone. It reads no other health or fitness data, and it never writes data to Health Connect.

How it is used

Your steps are converted into in-game progress (your "step pool"). That is the only use. Step data is not used for advertising, profiling, credit, insurance or any purpose other than running the game.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on your phone. The game stores your progress and per-day step totals in the app's local storage. Lantern Road has no account system and does not send your step data, or any other personal data, to a server or to third parties.

Removing your data

You can revoke step access at any time in Health Connect settings. Use Settings → Erase all progress in the game, or uninstall the app, to delete everything it has stored.

Children

Lantern Road is not directed at children under 13.
